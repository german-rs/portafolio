# What is germanriveros.cl?

germanriveros.cl is Germán Riveros' professional service for **administering, maintaining, optimizing and making accessible websites that already exist** — WordPress, Magento or any other platform.

It is deliberately **not** a from-scratch web development portfolio. The premise behind every piece of copy on the site is that the person reading it already has a website. What they need is someone who keeps it running: updated, secure, fast, and usable by everyone — including people who communicate in Chilean Sign Language (LSCh), an accessibility commitment that goes beyond code.

The site is also the first case study. Every technical decision documented in this repository — WCAG-validated contrast, `:focus-visible` parity with `:hover`, respect for `prefers-reduced-motion`, mobile tap-target sizing — is the same standard applied to client sites.

# Mission

Help people and organizations who already have a website keep it functioning: updated, secure, fast and accessible — so it keeps serving the people who depend on it, instead of quietly decaying.

# Vision

Become a trusted reference for web administration and maintenance, done with technical rigor and genuine human accessibility — evolving, as real client work justifies it, toward reusable components, templates and digital products, without losing the maintenance-first identity that makes the practice trustworthy.

# Core Values

## 🔧 Continuity

A website's launch is the beginning, not the end. Continuity — updates, backups, monitoring, timely response — is the actual product.

---

## 🚀 Performance

Performance is part of product quality from the very beginning.

Every intervention should measurably improve speed and efficiency, not just add features.

---

## ♿ Accessibility

Accessibility is validated in code — WCAG AA contrast, keyboard navigation, `prefers-reduced-motion` — and extended into human communication, including attention in Chilean Sign Language (LSCh) for clients and users who need it.

The web should be usable by as many people as possible, in as many ways as possible.

---

## 💎 Technical Quality

Projects are built using maintainable code, clean architecture and modern development practices, documented well enough that the reasoning survives beyond the person who wrote it.

---

## 📚 Continuous Learning

Web technologies and client needs evolve constantly.

Continuous learning allows new knowledge to become better solutions.

---

## 🤝 Transparency

Communicate clearly.

Explain technical decisions.

Build long-term trust — including an honest roadmap about what's still a placeholder.

# Philosophy

> **Technical Humanist.**

Technology is a means, not the objective. The objective is a website that keeps working for the people who actually use it — including the people the industry tends to design around, rather than for.
